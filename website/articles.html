RCC Pipe Guides & Technical Articles

Plain-language guides on RCC Hume pipes, IS 458:2021 pipe classes, indicative prices, culvert selection and installation — written for contractors, panchayat engineers and builders across Karnataka.

Last updated: July 2026

BKN Group of Industries has been manufacturing RCC spun pipes at Kunigal since 1998, with a second unit at Tiptur, and every week we answer the same practical questions from contractors and gram panchayat engineers: Which pipe class do I need — NP2 or NP3? What diameter suits my culvert? What should a fair ex-factory rate look like this season? These guides put those answers in one place. Everything here is written in plain Indian English, based on the requirements of IS 458:2021 (Precast Concrete Pipes) and on nearly three decades of casting, curing and despatching pipes from our own yards — our pipes are manufactured as per IS 458 specifications with strict in-house quality control over batching, water curing and dimensional inspection. Where we quote prices, they are clearly labelled indicative, because cement and steel rates move every month. Start with the buying guides below, or jump straight to our product pages for sizes and availability.
