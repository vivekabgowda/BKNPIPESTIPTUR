The House of BKN
Three units. One standard. Every pipe, pole and tank leaves our yards built to outlast the road above it.

Established in 1998 at Kunigal, BKN Group of Industries has grown into one of the region's trusted names in concrete manufacturing. From our units at Kunigal and Tiptur, we produce RCC spun pipes as per IS 458 specifications, prestressed concrete electric poles, underground water tanks and precast products — serving contractors, gram panchayats and government departments across Tumkur, Hassan, Chikmagalur, Chitradurga and beyond.
